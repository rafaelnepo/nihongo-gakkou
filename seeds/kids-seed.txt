ICONOTES KIDS — PERSONA SEED  (does NOT ship — its only job is to be the P3 identity source)
See personas.md → "The seed plan". Method from house-style.md. Written 2026-08-28.

WHY THIS EXISTS: the kids songs are lead + children's chorus + call-and-response. The
CHORUS is the poison for a Persona source (mixed vocal identity averages badly —
house-style.md ⚠, F-04/F-07), so the seed is ONE solo voice. But a Persona carries
INSTRUMENTATION as a tendency, and the toy-box palette (xylophone/marimba/toy piano/
ukulele/claps/woodblock) IS part of P3's identity — so KEEP those in the seed. What's held
back for per-episode is ONLY the kids chorus + call-and-response (the ~104 BPM episode
prompt in each animals song.json styles[]). Rule of thumb: sparse in VOICE, rich in the
palette we want carried.

RULES: ONE voice — no chorus, no second singer, no register jumps. Emotionally neutral,
all hiragana (F-02 not needed here — no particles). Dry, clear, playful. Never released.
