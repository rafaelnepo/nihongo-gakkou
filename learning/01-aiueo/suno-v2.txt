あいうえお — SUNO SCORE v2  (UNTESTED as of 2026-08-27)
learning/ strand — PUBLIC. Gojuon ROW 1 (あ行). CONTEXTUAL version.

WHY v2 (findings F-10): v1 was words-only and Nepô found (a) same-onset word
lists BLEND — "あ あさ あめ あお" runs together — and (b) words with no context
don't teach production. v2 fixes both: each word is SAID, then USED in a short
sentence, and words RECOMBINE across the song. It also reads as a tiny day:
morning → home & dog → out to the sea → an errand → a snack.

PATTERN per word:  the WORD (isolated, clear)  →  a short SENTENCE using it.
The sentence after a word never starts with that same word, so no two look-alike
lines are adjacent (F-08). The row refrain あいうえお closes every verse (F-07).

PHONETIC FIXES (findings F-02) — v2 HAS PARTICLES, so unlike v1 it needs them:
  は (topic) → ワ      を → を      へ (direction) → エ
  Only the PARTICLE changes: はらう / はやい keep their は. Applied below.
LONG VOWEL (F-05): おはよう — if the う is articulated as its own syllable
instead of lengthening (o-ha-yo-U), and ONLY then, change to おはよー here.
ALL HIRAGANA, no kanji — this is a kana-reading song.

DURATION: aim ~3:00 (Custom). If one pass won't reach it, use Suno EXTEND
(F-09) — never split-and-merge.

[A bright, clear solo vocal. Every syllable crisply enunciated. Tempo ~92.
 Warm and friendly, like a patient teacher. No ad-libs.]
