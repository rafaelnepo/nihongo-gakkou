Taught not as bare vowels but through small, high-frequency daily words in context — say the word, then hear it used. It reads as a tiny day: morning at home with the dog, then out to the sea, an errand at the station, and a snack to finish.

01

The idea

Words alone teach recognition, not production — and a list of same-onset words even blends together (findings F-10). So every target word is shown in a short sentence, and the words recombine across the song (いく is taught with いえ, then reused at the sea and the station).

The five-kana row あ い う え お closes every verse as the sung-together refrain — where Suno's unison habit recruits the listener (F-07). The 3-minute cut reaches length by adding a day bridge and a review pass, not by looping (F-12).
