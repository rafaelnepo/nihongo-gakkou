あいうえお — LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 1 (あ行). CONTEXTUAL version (v2). Written 2026-08-27.

CONCEPT: say the word, then see it USED. Each あ-row word is taught through a
short, natural sentence, and the words recombine across the song into a tiny
day — morning → home → out → errand → snack. Words-only was tried first (v1) and
Nepô found it blends and doesn't teach production (findings F-10); v2 is context
from the start. All hiragana — the point is reading kana; kanji shown here only
for meaning.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
あ  ── MORNING
  あさ        (asa)   morning (朝)
     → おはよう              (ohayō)                 good morning
  あめ        (ame)   rain (雨)
     → あさ から あめ        (asa kara ame)          rain since morning
  あお        (ao)    blue (青)
     → あめ の あと、そら は あおい
                              (ame no ato, sora wa aoi)   after the rain, the sky is blue

い  ── HOME & THE DOG
  いえ        (ie)    house (家)
     → ただいま              (tadaima)               I'm home
  いぬ        (inu)   dog (犬)
     → いえ に いぬ が いる  (ie ni inu ga iru)      there's a dog at home
  いく        (iku)   to go (行く)
     → いぬ と さんぽ に いく (inu to sanpo ni iku)   go for a walk with the dog

う  ── OUT TO THE SEA
  うみ        (umi)   sea (海)
     → うみ へ いく          (umi e iku)             go to the sea
  うた        (uta)   song (歌)
     → うた を うたう        (uta o utau)            sing a song
  うさぎ      (usagi) rabbit (兎)
     → うさぎ が いる        (usagi ga iru)          there's a rabbit

え  ── AT THE STATION
  えき        (eki)   station (駅)
     → えき へ いく          (eki e iku)             go to the station
  えんぴつ    (enpitsu) pencil (鉛筆)
     → えんぴつ を かう      (enpitsu o kau)         buy a pencil
  えん        (en)    yen (円)
     → えんぴつ は ひゃくえん (enpitsu wa hyaku-en)   the pencil is 100 yen

お  ── A SNACK
  おにぎり    (onigiri) rice ball (おにぎり)
     → おにぎり を たべる    (onigiri o taberu)      eat a rice ball
  おちゃ      (ocha)  tea (お茶)
     → おちゃ を のむ        (ocha o nomu)           drink tea
  おかね      (okane) money (お金)
     → おかね を はらう      (okane o harau)         pay the money

REFRAIN
  あ い う え お  (a i u e o)   the あ-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WHAT THIS SONG QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 NOUNS:
· Verbs (dictionary form, all high-frequency): いく go, いる be/exist, うたう
  sing, かう buy, たべる eat, のむ drink, はらう pay.
· Particles: から (from/since), の (of), に (at/to), と (with), へ (to/toward,
  read "e"), を (object, read "o"), は (topic, read "wa").
· Extra everyday nouns met in context: さんぽ (a walk/stroll), ひゃく (hundred).
· Word recombination: いく recurs (いぬと さんぽに〜, うみへ〜, えきへ〜); いる
  recurs (いぬ, うさぎ). Seeing a word again in a new sentence is the practice.

These are context (comprehensible input), not the test — the learner isn't
expected to parse every particle on day one. The TARGET is the あ-row words; the
sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 above (learn) → a BRIDGE that strings the words
into one day → a REVIEW of the sentences only (sing along). The short vertical
cut is PART 1 only. A few extra connective forms appear in the bridge/review:
  かえって   (kaette)   …return home AND (て-form, "and then")
  だよ       (da yo)    …it is / (casual "it's ~")
  です       (desu)     …is / it's (polite) — ひゃくえん です = "it's 100 yen"
The day (bridge), in order: 朝 morning → 家 home & dog → 海 sea → 駅 station →
snack. Same words, now flowing as a scene — the little RPG-rich payoff.
