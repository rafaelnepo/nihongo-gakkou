01

The idea

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every target sits in a small image — うみ の なみ, そら の にじ, き の ねっこ — and it recombines with earlier rows (うみ, そら, あさ, はな, はる, きいろい, たいよう return), keeping the ladder (F-13).

The five-kana row な に ぬ ね の closes every verse as the sung-together refrain (F-07). The 3-minute cut adds a day-in-nature bridge and a sentences-only review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the nature variation — the calm one, for koto × modern. Honest note: ぬ is the gojuon's hardest kana, so its verse reaches a little (a marsh, mud, the sun's warmth), and ね uses roots, a pussy willow and a roost. The mood carries the reach.
