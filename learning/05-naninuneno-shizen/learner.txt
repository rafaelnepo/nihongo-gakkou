なにぬねの（しぜん）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 5 (な行). NATURE variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the thing, then see it in the scene. Each な-row word is taught through a short
sentence, gathered into one quiet day outdoors — summer and sea, light and the garden, a
marsh and warmth, roots and a field. A calm sibling of the home and animals cuts. Recombines
earlier rows (うみ, そら, あさ, はな, はる, きいろい, たいよう) — the F-13 ladder. All
hiragana; kanji here only for meaning.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
な  ── SUMMER & SEA
  なつ        (natsu)  summer (夏)
     → あつい なつ          (atsui natsu)           a hot summer
  なみ        (nami)   wave (波)
     → うみ の なみ          (umi no nami)           waves on the sea
  なのはな    (nanohana) canola flower (菜の花)
     → きいろい なのはな    (kiiroi nanohana)       yellow canola flowers

に  ── LIGHT & THE GARDEN
  にじ        (niji)   rainbow (虹)
     → そら の にじ          (sora no niji)          a rainbow in the sky
  にわ        (niwa)   garden (庭)
     → はな の にわ          (hana no niwa)          a flower garden
  にっこう    (nikkō)  sunlight (日光)
     → あさ の にっこう      (asa no nikkō)          morning sunlight

ぬ  ── MARSH & WARMTH   (ぬ is the gojuon's hardest kana — nature reaches for it)
  ぬま        (numa)   marsh / pond (沼)
     → しずかな ぬま        (shizuka na numa)       a quiet marsh
  ぬかるみ    (nukarumi) mud (ぬかるみ)
     → あめ の ぬかるみ      (ame no nukarumi)       mud after rain
  ぬくもり    (nukumori) warmth (ぬくもり)
     → たいよう の ぬくもり  (taiyō no nukumori)     the sun's warmth

ね  ── ROOTS & BRANCHES
  ねっこ      (nekko)  roots (根っこ)
     → き の ねっこ          (ki no nekko)           a tree's roots
  ねこやなぎ  (nekoyanagi) pussy willow (猫柳)
     → はる の ねこやなぎ    (haru no nekoyanagi)    spring pussy willows
  ねぐら      (negura) roost (ねぐら)
     → とり の ねぐら        (tori no negura)        a bird's roost

の  ── FIELDS & HILLS
  のはら      (nohara) field (野原)
     → ひろい のはら        (hiroi nohara)          a wide field
  のぎく      (nogiku) wild daisy (野菊)
     → しろい のぎく        (shiroi nogiku)         white wild daisies
  のやま      (noyama) hills & fields (野山)
     → とおい のやま        (tōi noyama)            distant hills

REFRAIN
  な に ぬ ね の  (na ni nu ne no)   the な-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Adjectives: あつい hot, きいろい yellow, しずかな quiet, しろい white, ひろい wide, とおい far.
· Nouns met in context: うみ sea, そら sky, はな flower, き tree, とり bird, たいよう the sun.
· Particles: の (of) — the whole song is built on it (a quiet, descriptive grammar).
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: うみ, そら, あさ, はな, はる, たいよう all return.

The TARGET is the な-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 (learn) → a BRIDGE through one day in nature → a REVIEW of
the sentences only (sing along).

SIBLINGS (same five kana): 05-naninuneno-ouchi (home) and 05-naninuneno-doubutsu (animals).
Sing all three to read な行 cold.
