はひふへほ（しぜん）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 6 (は行). NATURE variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the thing, then see it in the scene. Each は-row word is taught through a short
sentence, gathered into one quiet day outdoors — flowers and woods, light and sun, mountain
and season, stars and flame. A calm sibling of the making and animals cuts. Recombines
earlier rows (つき, なつ, よる, くさ) — the F-13 ladder. All hiragana; kanji here only for meaning.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
は  ── FLOWERS & WOODS
  はな        (hana)   flower (花)
     → あかい はな          (akai hana)             a red flower
  はっぱ      (happa)  leaf (葉っぱ)
     → みどりの はっぱ      (midori no happa)       green leaves
  はやし      (hayashi) woods / grove (林)
     → しずかな はやし      (shizuka na hayashi)    a quiet woods

ひ  ── LIGHT & SUN
  ひかり      (hikari) light (光)
     → つき の ひかり        (tsuki no hikari)       moonlight
  ひまわり    (himawari) sunflower (向日葵)
     → きいろい ひまわり    (kiiroi himawari)       a yellow sunflower
  ひ          (hi)     sun / fire (日 / 火)
     → あかい ひ            (akai hi)               the red setting sun

ふ  ── MOUNTAIN & SEASON
  ふじ        (fuji)   Mt. Fuji (富士)
     → たかい ふじ          (takai fuji)            tall Mt. Fuji
  ふゆ        (fuyu)   winter (冬)
     → さむい ふゆ          (samui fuyu)            a cold winter
  ふうりん    (fūrin)  wind chime (風鈴)
     → なつ の ふうりん      (natsu no fūrin)        a summer wind chime

へ  ── GRASS & PLAIN   (へ is a thin row — a snake, a loofah, a plain carry it)
  へちま      (hechima) loofah gourd (へちま)
     → みどりの へちま      (midori no hechima)     a green loofah
  へび        (hebi)   snake (蛇)
     → くさ の へび          (kusa no hebi)          a snake in the grass
  へいや      (heiya)  a plain / flatland (平野)
     → ひろい へいや        (hiroi heiya)           a wide plain

ほ  ── STARS & FLAME
  ほし        (hoshi)  star (星)
     → よる の ほし          (yoru no hoshi)         stars at night
  ほたる      (hotaru) firefly (蛍)
     → なつ の ほたる        (natsu no hotaru)       summer fireflies
  ほのお      (honō)   flame (炎)
     → たきび の ほのお      (takibi no honō)        a bonfire's flame

REFRAIN
  は ひ ふ へ ほ  (ha hi fu he ho)   the は-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Adjectives: あかい red, みどりの green, しずかな quiet, きいろい yellow, たかい tall,
  さむい cold, ひろい wide.
· Nouns met in context: つき moon, くさ grass, なつ summer, よる night, たきび bonfire.
· Particle: の (of) — the whole song is built on it.
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: つき (た行), なつ, よる, くさ (か行) all return.

The TARGET is the は-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 (learn) → a BRIDGE through one day in nature → a REVIEW of
the sentences only (sing along).

SIBLINGS (same five kana): 06-hahifuheho-gakkou (making) and 06-hahifuheho-doubutsu (animals).
Sing all three to read は行 cold.
