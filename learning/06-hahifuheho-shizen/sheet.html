01

The idea

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every target sits in a small image — つき の ひかり, たかい ふじ, なつ の ほたる — and it recombines with earlier rows (つき, なつ, よる, くさ, きいろい, あかい return), keeping the ladder (F-13).

The five-kana row は ひ ふ へ ほ closes every verse as the sung-together refrain (F-07). The 3-minute cut adds a day-in-nature bridge and a sentences-only review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the nature variation — the calm one, for koto × modern. は行 has the sky-and-season words (ひまわり, ふじ, ふゆ, ほし, ほたる) that suit it. Honest note: へ is a thin row, carried by a garden snake, a loofah and a wide plain.
