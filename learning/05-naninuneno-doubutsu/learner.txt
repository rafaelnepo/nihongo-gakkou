なにぬねの（どうぶつ）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 5 (な行). ANIMALS variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the animal, then see it DO something. Each な-row word is taught through a short
sentence, gathered into a field-and-zoo day. One of THREE な-row songs (home · nature ·
animals). Recombines earlier rows (くま, かえる, はっぱ, とり + verbs ねる・はしる・はねる・
いる) — the F-13 ladder. All hiragana; kanji here only for meaning.

HONEST NOTE: な行 is a hard row for animals. ぬ has NO beginner animal at all (the whole
gojuon's toughest kana), so its verse uses a stuffed animal (ぬいぐるみ), a pond frog, and a
soft cloth. な and に reach a little too (a slug, a macaque, a python). The home and nature
cuts are the cleaner な-row songs.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
な  ── SLOW & SMALL
  なまず      (namazu) catfish (鯰)
     → なまず が およぐ      (namazu ga oyogu)       a catfish swims
  なまけもの  (namakemono) sloth (なまけもの)
     → なまけもの が ねる    (namakemono ga neru)    a sloth sleeps
  なめくじ    (namekuji) slug (なめくじ)
     → はっぱ の なめくじ    (happa no namekuji)     a slug on a leaf

に  ── THE FARM & JUNGLE
  にわとり    (niwatori) chicken (鶏)
     → にわとり が なく      (niwatori ga naku)      a chicken clucks
  にほんざる  (nihonzaru) Japanese macaque (日本猿)
     → にほんざる が いる    (nihonzaru ga iru)      there's a Japanese monkey
  にしきへび  (nishikihebi) python (錦蛇)
     → にしきへび は ながい  (nishikihebi wa nagai)  the python is long

ぬ  ── NO ぬ ANIMAL — A DETOUR   (ぬ is the gojuon's hardest kana; no beginner animal exists)
  ぬいぐるみ  (nuigurumi) stuffed animal (ぬいぐるみ)
     → くまの ぬいぐるみ    (kuma no nuigurumi)     a teddy bear
  ぬま        (numa)   pond / marsh (沼)
     → ぬま の かえる        (numa no kaeru)         a frog in the pond
  ぬの        (nuno)   cloth (布)
     → ふわふわの ぬの      (fuwafuwa no nuno)      a fluffy cloth (a pet's blanket)

ね  ── CAT & MOUSE
  ねこ        (neko)   cat (猫)
     → ねこ が ねる          (neko ga neru)          a cat sleeps
  ねずみ      (nezumi) mouse (鼠)
     → ねずみ が はしる      (nezumi ga hashiru)     a mouse runs
  ねぐら      (negura) roost / den (ねぐら)
     → とり の ねぐら        (tori no negura)        a bird's roost

の  ── OUT IN THE FIELD
  のねずみ    (nonezumi) field mouse (野鼠)
     → のねずみ が いる      (nonezumi ga iru)       there's a field mouse
  のうさぎ    (nousagi) hare (野兎)
     → のうさぎ が はねる    (nousagi ga haneru)     a hare hops
  のらねこ    (noraneko) stray cat (野良猫)
     → のらねこ が あるく    (noraneko ga aruku)     a stray cat walks

REFRAIN
  な に ぬ ね の  (na ni nu ne no)   the な-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Verbs: およぐ swim, ねる sleep, なく cluck/cry, いる be, はしる run, はねる hop, あるく walk.
· Adjective: ながい long. Particles: が (subject), は (topic, "wa"), の (of).
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: くま (か行), かえる (か行), はっぱ, とり (た行) all return.
· Note the の verse: のねずみ / のうさぎ / のらねこ are all the "wild / field" (野) versions —
  a tidy little pattern.

The TARGET is the な-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 (learn) → a field-friends BRIDGE → a REVIEW of the
sentences only (sing along).

SIBLINGS (same five kana): 05-naninuneno-ouchi (home) and 05-naninuneno-shizen (nature) —
the cleaner two な-row cuts. Sing all three to read な行 cold.
