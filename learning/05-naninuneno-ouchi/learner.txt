なにぬねの（おうち）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 5 (な行). HOME variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the thing, then USE it. Each な-row word is taught through a short sentence,
gathered into a fun day at home — cook, garden, play, the cat, a snack. One of THREE な-row
songs (home · nature · animals). Recombines earlier rows (くま, ねる, あそぶ, おにぎり, あまい,
あおい) — the F-13 ladder. All hiragana; kanji here only for meaning.

NOTE: ぬ is the hardest kana in the whole gojuon (almost no beginner nouns), and the home
cut is where it fits BEST — its words are the toy-box ones (ぬいぐるみ, ぬりえ, ぬの).

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
な  ── IN THE KITCHEN
  なべ        (nabe)   pot (鍋)
     → なべ で つくる        (nabe de tsukuru)       cook in a pot
  なす        (nasu)   eggplant (茄子)
     → むらさきの なす      (murasaki no nasu)      a purple eggplant
  なし        (nashi)  pear (梨)
     → あまい なし          (amai nashi)            a sweet pear

に  ── COOKING & THE GARDEN
  にく        (niku)   meat (肉)
     → にく を やく          (niku o yaku)           grill meat
  にんじん    (ninjin) carrot (人参)
     → にんじん を きる      (ninjin o kiru)         cut a carrot
  にわ        (niwa)   garden (庭)
     → にわ の はな          (niwa no hana)          garden flowers

ぬ  ── THE TOY BOX   (ぬ is the gojuon's hardest kana — its everyday words are toys)
  ぬいぐるみ  (nuigurumi) stuffed toy (ぬいぐるみ)
     → くまの ぬいぐるみ    (kuma no nuigurumi)     a teddy bear
  ぬりえ      (nurie)  colouring book (塗り絵)
     → ぬりえ を する        (nurie o suru)          do some colouring
  ぬの        (nuno)   cloth (布)
     → あおい ぬの          (aoi nuno)              blue cloth

ね  ── THE CAT & CLAY
  ねこ        (neko)   cat (猫)
     → ねこ が ねる          (neko ga neru)          a cat sleeps
  ねぎ        (negi)   green onion (葱)
     → ねぎ を きる          (negi o kiru)           cut green onion
  ねんど      (nendo)  clay (粘土)
     → ねんど で あそぶ      (nendo de asobu)        play with clay

の  ── A SNACK & OUT
  のり        (nori)   seaweed (海苔)
     → のり の おにぎり      (nori no onigiri)       a seaweed rice ball
  のみもの    (nomimono) a drink (飲み物)
     → つめたい のみもの    (tsumetai nomimono)     a cold drink
  のはら      (nohara) field (野原)
     → ひろい のはら        (hiroi nohara)          a wide field

REFRAIN
  な に ぬ ね の  (na ni nu ne no)   the な-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Verbs: つくる make/cook, やく grill/bake, きる cut, する do, ねる sleep, あそぶ play.
· Adjectives: むらさきの purple, あまい sweet, あおい blue, つめたい cold, ひろい wide.
· Particles: で (with/at), の (of), を (object, "o"), が (subject).
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: くま (か行), ねる (あ行 animals), あそぶ, おにぎり (あ行),
  あまい, あおい all return.

The TARGET is the な-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 (learn) → a BRIDGE through one day at home → a REVIEW of
the sentences only (sing along).

SIBLINGS (same five kana): 05-naninuneno-shizen (nature) and 05-naninuneno-doubutsu
(animals). Sing all three to read な行 cold.
