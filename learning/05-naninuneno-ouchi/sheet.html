Row 5 of the gojuon — な に ぬ ね の — through a fun day at home: cook in a pot, grill meat, cuddle a teddy bear, colour a picture, play with clay, the cat naps. Say the thing, then use it. The home variation of three な-row songs (home · nature · animals).

01

The idea

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every target sits in a small action — にく を やく, ぬりえ を する, ねんど で あそぶ — and it recombines with earlier rows (くま, ねる, あそぶ, おにぎり, あまい, あおい return), keeping the ladder (F-13).

The five-kana row な に ぬ ね の closes every verse as the sung-together refrain (F-07). The 3-minute cut adds a day-at-home bridge and a sentences-only review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the home variation. Note: ぬ is the hardest kana in the whole gojuon (almost no beginner nouns), and the home cut is where it fits best — its words are the toy-box ones: ぬいぐるみ (a stuffed toy), ぬりえ (a colouring book), ぬの (cloth). Pairs with the Female (city pop) voice.
