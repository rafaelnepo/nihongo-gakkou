The idea

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every target sits in a small image — あめ が ふる, いけ の みず, あおい うみ — and the sentences reuse words from 01-aiueo (あめ, あお→あおい, あさ, うみ), so the song is comprehensible because of the first (F-13).

The five-kana row あ い う え お closes every verse as the sung-together refrain (F-07). The 3-minute cut adds a seasons-walk bridge and a sentences-only review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the nature variation — the calm one, for a koto × modern arrangement. Honest note: あ行's え is a hard row (few beginner nature nouns), so its verse leans on trees and water — えだ (branch) · え (a cove) · えのき (a big tree).
