あいうえお（しぜん）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 1 (あ行). NATURE variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the thing, then see it in the scene. Each あ-row word is taught through a
short, natural sentence, and the words gather into one quiet walk outdoors. A calm
sibling of the original 01-aiueo (a little day) — same five kana, fresh words — that
deliberately REUSES words from 01-aiueo (あめ, あお→あおい, あさ→あさひ, うみ) as known
ground (the F-13 ladder). All hiragana — the point is reading kana; kanji shown here
only for meaning.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
あ  ── SKY & RAIN
  あめ        (ame)    rain (雨)   ← from 01-aiueo
     → あめ が ふる          (ame ga furu)           rain falls
  あさひ      (asahi)  morning sun (朝日)
     → あさひ が のぼる      (asahi ga noboru)       the morning sun rises
  あき        (aki)    autumn (秋)
     → あき の はっぱ        (aki no happa)          autumn leaves

い  ── WATER & STONE
  いけ        (ike)    pond (池)
     → いけ の みず          (ike no mizu)           the pond's water
  いし        (ishi)   stone (石)
     → おおきい いし        (ōkii ishi)             a big stone
  いずみ      (izumi)  spring / fountain (泉)
     → つめたい いずみ      (tsumetai izumi)        a cold spring

う  ── THE SEA
  うみ        (umi)    sea (海)   ← from 01-aiueo
     → あおい うみ          (aoi umi)               the blue sea
  うめ        (ume)    plum blossom (梅)
     → うめ の はな          (ume no hana)           plum blossoms
  うず        (uzu)    swirl / eddy (渦)
     → みず の うず          (mizu no uzu)           a swirl of water

え  ── BRANCHES & A COVE   (あ行's hard row — few beginner え nature nouns)
  えだ        (eda)    branch (枝)
     → き の えだ            (ki no eda)             a tree's branch
  え          (e)      cove / inlet (江)
     → しずかな え          (shizuka na e)          a quiet cove
  えのき      (enoki)  hackberry tree (榎)
     → おおきい えのき      (ōkii enoki)            a big hackberry tree

お  ── HILLS & SKY
  おか        (oka)    hill (丘)
     → みどりの おか        (midori no oka)         a green hill
  おおぞら    (ōzora)  the big sky (大空)
     → あおい おおぞら      (aoi ōzora)             the big blue sky
  おちば      (ochiba) fallen leaves (落ち葉)
     → あき の おちば        (aki no ochiba)         autumn fallen leaves

REFRAIN
  あ い う え お  (a i u e o)   the あ-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WHAT THIS SONG QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Verbs: ふる fall (rain/snow), のぼる rise/climb.
· Adjectives: おおきい big, つめたい cold, あおい blue, みどりの green, しずかな quiet.
· Nouns met in context: みず (water), はな (flower), き (tree), はっぱ (leaf),
  はる (spring), いろ (colour).
· Particles: が (subject), の (of).
· Word recombination WITH 01-aiueo: あめ (rain), あお→あおい (blue), あさ→あさひ
  (morning→morning sun), うみ (sea) all return — that re-meeting is why this song is
  easy after the first (F-13).

These are context, not the test — the TARGET is the あ-row words; the sentence, the
audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 above (learn) → a BRIDGE that walks the scenes into
one morning outdoors → a REVIEW of the sentences only (sing along). A couple of extra
words appear in the bridge: はる (spring), いろ (colour).

SIBLINGS (same five kana, different worlds): 01-aiueo (the original — a little day) and
01-aiueo-doubutsu (animals). Sing all three to read あ行 cold.
