あいうえお（しぜん）— STYLE NOTES

THE CALM CUT. This nature variation is the one to render on KOTO × MODERN rather than
city pop — it suits the quiet outdoor scenes and gives the three あ-row siblings sonic
variety (the original 01-aiueo and the animals cut are city pop). Still the house
FEMALE voice (F-11), same Persona, just a calmer arrangement. Custom mode; do NOT
specify gender on top of the Persona.

RECOMMENDED PROMPT (song.json styles[0]):
  modern Japanese pop with traditional koto, 90 BPM, single clear female vocalist,
  koto, shakuhachi accents, soft modern beat, warm bass, elegant and calm, dry forward
  vocal, extremely clear enunciation, no ad-libs.

TEMPO: ~90 — a hair under the 92 floor (F-01) is fine for the calm koto cut; keep it
sing-along-able on first listen.

Alternatives kept for other content: city pop (styles[1]), acoustic, lo-fi. Full
strings in song.json.styles[].
