さしすせそ（ごはん）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 3 (さ行). MEALTIME variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the food, then USE it. Each さ-row word is taught through a short sentence,
and the words gather into a day of meals — shop, cook, eat, sit at the table, and a cozy
close at grandma's. One of THREE さ-row songs (mealtime · nature · animals). Recombines
earlier rows (かう, あまい, そら) — the F-13 ladder. All hiragana; kanji here only for
meaning.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
さ  ── SHOPPING
  さいふ      (saifu)  wallet (財布)
     → さいふ を もつ        (saifu o motsu)         bring the wallet
  さかな      (sakana) fish (魚)
     → さかな を かう        (sakana o kau)          buy fish
  さとう      (satō)   sugar (砂糖)
     → さとう は あまい      (satō wa amai)          sugar is sweet

し  ── COOKING
  しお        (shio)   salt (塩)
     → しお を いれる        (shio o ireru)          add salt
  しる        (shiru)  soup (汁)
     → あつい しる          (atsui shiru)           hot soup
  しめじ      (shimeji) shimeji mushroom (しめじ)
     → しめじ を たべる      (shimeji o taberu)      eat the mushrooms

す  ── EATING
  すし        (sushi)  sushi (寿司)
     → おいしい すし        (oishii sushi)          delicious sushi
  すいか      (suika)  watermelon (すいか)
     → あまい すいか        (amai suika)            sweet watermelon
  す          (su)     vinegar (酢)
     → す は すっぱい        (su wa suppai)          vinegar is sour

せ  ── AT THE TABLE
  せんべい    (senbei) rice cracker (煎餅)
     → せんべい を たべる    (senbei o taberu)       eat a rice cracker
  せき        (seki)   seat (席)
     → せき に すわる        (seki ni suwaru)        sit in your seat
  せっけん    (sekken) soap (石鹸)
     → せっけん で あらう    (sekken de arau)        wash with soap

そ  ── A COZY CLOSE
  そば        (soba)   soba noodles (そば)
     → あたたかい そば      (atatakai soba)         warm soba
  そぼ        (sobo)   grandmother (祖母)
     → そぼ の ごはん        (sobo no gohan)         grandma's cooking
  そら        (sora)   sky (空)
     → ゆうやけ の そら      (yūyake no sora)        the sunset sky

REFRAIN
  さ し す せ そ  (sa shi su se so)   the さ-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Verbs: もつ hold/bring, かう buy, いれる put in, たべる eat, すわる sit, あらう wash.
· Adjectives (tastes!): あまい sweet, あつい hot, おいしい delicious, すっぱい sour,
  あたたかい warm.
· Particles: を (object, "o"), は (topic, "wa"), に (in/at), で (with/by), の (of).
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: かう (from か行), あまい (from the nature cuts), そら
  (from あ行) return — that re-meeting is the practice (F-13).

The TARGET is the さ-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 (learn) → a BRIDGE that strings the meal into one day →
a REVIEW of the sentences only (sing along). Extra words in the bridge: かいもの (shopping),
つくる (to make). Note the wordplay in the close — そぼ の そば = "beside grandma", the same
そば as the noodles.

SIBLINGS (same five kana): 03-sashisuseso-shizen (nature) and 03-sashisuseso-doubutsu
(animals). Sing all three to read さ行 cold.
