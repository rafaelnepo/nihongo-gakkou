Row 3 of the gojuon — さ し す せ そ — taught through a day of meals: shop for fish and sugar, salt the soup, eat sushi and watermelon, sit at the table, and finish with warm soba at grandma's under the evening sky. Say the food, then use it. The mealtime variation of three さ-row songs (mealtime · nature · animals).

City pop · female (house voice)~92 BPMComprehensible inputYouTube ~3:00 · vertical ~1:00
01

The idea

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every target sits in a small action — さかな を かう, しお を いれる, せき に すわる — and it recombines with earlier rows (かう from か行, あまい from nature, たべる/あらう, そら from あ行), so さ行 keeps the ladder going (F-13).

The five-kana row さ し す せ そ closes every verse as the sung-together refrain (F-07). The 3-minute cut adds a meal-day bridge and a sentences-only review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the mealtime variation — さ行 is unusually rich in food and household words, so the everyday cut is a kitchen-and-table day. Sing it beside the nature and animals cuts to read さ行 cold.
