Row 9 of the gojuon — ら り る れ ろ — through everyday things: listen to the radio, a bright lamp, a red apple, cooking, a cold fridge and a sour lemon, light a candle, a rocket flies. Say the thing, then use it. The home variation of three ら-row songs. ら行 is the modern-objects row — lots of familiar loanwords, read here in kana.

01

The idea

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every target sits in a small action — らじお を きく, りょうり を する, ろうそく を つける — recombining earlier rows (きく, ふく, する, つめたい, すっぱい return; F-13). The row closes every verse (F-07); the longer cut adds a bridge + review (F-12).

The five-kana row ら り る れ ろ is the sung-together refrain. ら行 is full of everyday and imported words (らじお, ろぼっと, ろけっと) — written in hiragana here, since the point is reading the kana.

Honest note: る is one of the gojuon's hardest kana (only a handful of beginner words), so its verse leans on being-out (るす), a magnifier (るーぺ) and a ruby (るびー). Pairs with the Female (city pop) voice.
