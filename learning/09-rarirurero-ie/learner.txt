らりるれろ（いえ）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 9 (ら行). HOME / THINGS variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the thing, then USE it. Each ら-row word is taught through a short sentence,
gathered into a day with everyday things — sound & light, a snack, the kitchen, a candle & a
rocket. One of THREE ら-row songs (things · animals · colours). ら行 is the modern-objects
row, so many words are familiar loanwords, written in hiragana here (the skill is reading
kana). Recombines earlier rows (きく, ふく, する, つめたい, すっぱい) — the F-13 ladder.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ら  ── SOUND & LIGHT
  らじお      (rajio)  radio (ラジオ)
     → らじお を きく        (rajio o kiku)          listen to the radio
  らんぷ      (ranpu)  lamp (ランプ)
     → あかるい らんぷ      (akarui ranpu)          a bright lamp
  らっぱ      (rappa)  trumpet (ラッパ)
     → らっぱ を ふく        (rappa o fuku)          blow a trumpet

り  ── APPLE & RIBBON
  りんご      (ringo)  apple (林檎)
     → あかい りんご        (akai ringo)            a red apple
  りぼん      (ribon)  ribbon (リボン)
     → かわいい りぼん      (kawaii ribon)          a cute ribbon
  りょうり    (ryōri)  cooking (料理)
     → りょうり を する      (ryōri o suru)          do the cooking

る  ── OUT & SHINY   (る is one of the gojuon's hardest kana — few beginner words)
  るす        (rusu)   being out / away (留守)
     → いえ が るす          (ie ga rusu)            nobody's home
  るーぺ      (rūpe)   magnifier (ルーペ)
     → るーぺ で みる        (rūpe de miru)          look with a magnifier
  るびー      (rubī)   ruby (ルビー)
     → ひかる るびー        (hikaru rubī)           a shiny ruby

れ  ── THE KITCHEN
  れいぞうこ  (reizōko) fridge (冷蔵庫)
     → つめたい れいぞうこ  (tsumetai reizōko)      a cold fridge
  れもん      (remon)  lemon (レモン)
     → すっぱい れもん      (suppai remon)          a sour lemon
  れんが      (renga)  brick (煉瓦)
     → かたい れんが        (katai renga)           a hard brick

ろ  ── CANDLE & ROCKET
  ろうそく    (rōsoku) candle (蝋燭)
     → ろうそく を つける    (rōsoku o tsukeru)      light a candle
  ろぼっと    (robotto) robot (ロボット)
     → おおきい ろぼっと    (ōkii robotto)          a big robot
  ろけっと    (roketto) rocket (ロケット)
     → ろけっと が とぶ      (roketto ga tobu)       a rocket flies

REFRAIN
  ら り る れ ろ  (ra ri ru re ro)   the ら-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Verbs: きく listen, ふく blow, する do, みる look, つける light/turn on.
· Adjectives: あかるい bright, あかい red, かわいい cute, ひかる shiny, つめたい cold,
  すっぱい sour, かたい hard, おおきい big.
· Particles: を (object, always "o"; never swap — F-16), の (of), が (subject).
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: きく, ふく, する, つめたい, すっぱい (さ行) all return.

The TARGET is the ら-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 (learn) → a BRIDGE through a day with things → a REVIEW of
the sentences only (sing along).

SIBLINGS (same five kana): 09-rarirurero-doubutsu (animals) and 09-rarirurero-iro (colours &
pretty things). Sing all three to read ら行 cold.
