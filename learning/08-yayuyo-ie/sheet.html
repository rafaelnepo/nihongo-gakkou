01

The idea

や行 is a short row — only three kana (や ゆ よ; the い/え slots are borrowed from あ行). So this song is 3 groups of 3 words (9 targets) instead of the usual 15, and the refrain is just や ゆ よ. Naturally a shorter song — the vertical cut is the sweet spot.

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every target sits in a small action — やさい を たべる, ゆのみ の おちゃ — recombining earlier rows (たべる, おちゃ, いえ return; F-13). The row closes every verse for the sung-together refrain (F-07); the longer cut adds a bridge + sentences-only review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the home variation. Pairs with the Female (city pop) voice.
