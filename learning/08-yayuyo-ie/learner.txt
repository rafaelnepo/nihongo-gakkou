やゆよ（いえ）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 8 (や行, a SHORT row). HOME variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the thing, then USE it. The ya-row has only THREE kana (や ゆ よ) — the い/え
slots are borrowed from あ行 — so this song teaches 3 groups of 3 words (9 targets) and the
refrain is や ゆ よ. Naturally a short song. One of THREE や-row songs (home · nature · a fun
day out). Recombines earlier rows (たべる, おちゃ, いえ) — the F-13 ladder. All hiragana.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
や  ── THE HOUSE
  やね        (yane)   roof (屋根)
     → あかい やね          (akai yane)             a red roof
  やかん      (yakan)  kettle (薬缶)
     → やかん の ゆげ        (yakan no yuge)         steam from the kettle
  やさい      (yasai)  vegetables (野菜)
     → やさい を たべる      (yasai o taberu)        eat vegetables

ゆ  ── HANDS & TEA
  ゆび        (yubi)   finger (指)
     → ちいさい ゆび        (chiisai yubi)          little fingers
  ゆか        (yuka)   floor (床)
     → きれいな ゆか        (kirei na yuka)         a clean floor
  ゆのみ      (yunomi) teacup (湯呑み)
     → ゆのみ の おちゃ      (yunomi no ocha)        tea in the teacup

よ  ── EVENING
  よる        (yoru)   night (夜)
     → しずかな よる        (shizuka na yoru)       a quiet night
  よこ        (yoko)   side / beside (横)
     → いえ の よこ          (ie no yoko)            beside the house
  ようふく    (yōfuku) clothes (洋服)
     → あたらしい ようふく  (atarashii yōfuku)      new clothes

REFRAIN
  や ゆ よ  (ya yu yo)   the ya-row (short)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 9 WORDS:
· Verb: たべる eat. Adjectives: あかい red, ちいさい small, きれいな clean, しずかな quiet,
  あたらしい new.
· Particles: を (object, always "o" — never swap it, F-16), の (of).
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: たべる, おちゃ (あ行), いえ (あ行) return.

The TARGET is the や-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE CUTS: v2 = the teach only (~0:45, a tidy vertical short). v3 = teach → bridge → review
(~2:00). A short row makes a short song — that's fine; the vertical cut is its home.

SIBLINGS (same three kana): 08-yayuyo-shizen (nature) and 08-yayuyo-asobi (a fun day out).
Sing all three to read や行 cold.
