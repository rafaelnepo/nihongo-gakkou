まみむめも（おうち）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 7 (ま行). HOME variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the thing, then USE it. Each ま-row word is taught through a short sentence,
gathered into a cosy day at home — the window, a snack, small things, glasses, a nap. One of
THREE ま-row songs (home · nature · animals). Recombines earlier rows (あける, たべる, うさぎ,
あまい, つめたい, あたたかい) — the F-13 ladder. All hiragana; kanji here only for meaning.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ま  ── THE WINDOW
  まど        (mado)   window (窓)
     → まど を あける        (mado o akeru)          open the window
  まくら      (makura) pillow (枕)
     → しろい まくら        (shiroi makura)         a white pillow
  まめ        (mame)   bean (豆)
     → まめ を たべる        (mame o taberu)         eat beans

み  ── A SNACK & WATER
  みかん      (mikan)  mandarin (蜜柑)
     → あまい みかん        (amai mikan)            a sweet mandarin
  みず        (mizu)   water (水)
     → つめたい みず        (tsumetai mizu)         cold water
  みみ        (mimi)   ear (耳)
     → うさぎ の みみ        (usagi no mimi)         a rabbit's ears

む  ── LITTLE THINGS
  むし        (mushi)  bug (虫)
     → ちいさい むし        (chiisai mushi)         a little bug
  むらさき    (murasaki) purple (紫)
     → むらさきの はな      (murasaki no hana)      a purple flower
  むぎ        (mugi)   barley (麦)
     → むぎ の ちゃ          (mugi no cha)           barley tea

め  ── EYES & GLASSES
  め          (me)     eye (目)
     → おおきい め          (ōkii me)               big eyes
  めがね      (megane) glasses (眼鏡)
     → まるい めがね        (marui megane)          round glasses
  めだか      (medaka) killifish (めだか)
     → めだか が およぐ      (medaka ga oyogu)       a killifish swims

も  ── A SWEET & A NAP
  もも        (momo)   peach (桃)
     → あまい もも          (amai momo)             a sweet peach
  もち        (mochi)  rice cake (餅)
     → しろい もち          (shiroi mochi)          white mochi
  もうふ      (mōfu)   blanket (毛布)
     → あたたかい もうふ    (atatakai mōfu)         a warm blanket

REFRAIN
  ま み む め も  (ma mi mu me mo)   the ま-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Verbs: あける open, たべる eat, およぐ swim.
· Adjectives: しろい white, あまい sweet, つめたい cold, ちいさい small, まるい round,
  おおきい big, あたたかい warm.
· Particles: を (object, always "o" — never swap it, F-16), の (of).
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: あける, たべる, うさぎ all return.

The TARGET is the ま-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 (learn) → a BRIDGE through one day at home → a REVIEW of
the sentences only (sing along).

SIBLINGS (same five kana): 07-mamimumemo-shizen (nature) and 07-mamimumemo-doubutsu (animals).
Sing all three to read ま行 cold.
