か行 ・ gojuon row 2 ・ a town day

learningwritten

Row 2 of the gojuon — か き く け こ — through the everyday things of a trip into town: an umbrella, a key, shoes, the car, a ticket, the park. Say the thing, then use it. The town variation of three か-row songs (animals · town · nature), the same five kana practised without a song repeating.

City pop · female (house voice)~92 BPMComprehensible inputYouTube ~3:00 · vertical ~1:00
01

The idea

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every target is taught in a small action — かさ を もつ, きっぷ を かう, くつ を はく — and it recombines with row 1 (あめ, いえ, そら, えき, えんぴつ and かう return), so か行 reviews あ行 as it goes.

The five-kana row か き く け こ closes every verse as the sung-together refrain (F-07). The 3-minute cut adds a town-day bridge and a sentences-only review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the town variation — the closest in spirit to 01-aiueo's 'little day'. It teaches the highest-utility everyday words of the three か-row songs; sing it beside the animals and nature cuts to read the row cold. The け verse is the loose 'small errands' slot (けしごむ・けいと・けさ), the same role え held in row 1.
