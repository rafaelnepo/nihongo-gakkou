かきくけこ（まち）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 2 (か行). TOWN variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the everyday thing, then USE it. Each か-row word is taught through a
short, natural sentence, and the words gather into a small day out — getting ready,
heading out, the ride, a couple of errands, the park. One of THREE か-row songs
(animals · town · nature) that teach the same five kana with different words, so the
row gets practised without a song repeating. Row 1's words come back (あめ, いえ, そら,
えき, えんぴつ, かう), so か行 also reviews あ行. All hiragana — the point is reading
kana; kanji shown here only for meaning.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
か  ── GETTING READY
  かお        (kao)    face (顔)
     → かお を あらう        (kao o arau)            wash your face
  かさ        (kasa)   umbrella (傘)
     → あめ だから かさ      (ame dakara kasa)       rain — so, an umbrella
  かぎ        (kagi)   key (鍵)
     → いえ の かぎ          (ie no kagi)            the house key

き  ── HEADING OUT
  きる        (kiru)   to wear (着る)
     → ふく を きる          (fuku o kiru)           put on clothes
  きいろ      (kiiro)  yellow (黄色)
     → きいろい ぼうし        (kiiroi bōshi)          a yellow hat
  きっぷ      (kippu)  ticket (切符)
     → きっぷ を かう        (kippu o kau)           buy a ticket

く  ── ON THE WAY
  くつ        (kutsu)  shoes (靴)
     → くつ を はく          (kutsu o haku)          put on your shoes
  くるま      (kuruma) car (車)
     → くるま に のる        (kuruma ni noru)        get in the car
  くも        (kumo)   cloud (雲)
     → そら に くも          (sora ni kumo)          a cloud in the sky

け  ── LITTLE ERRANDS   (the loose slot — same role え held in row 1)
  けしごむ    (keshigomu) eraser (消しゴム)
     → えんぴつ と けしごむ  (enpitsu to keshigomu)  a pencil and an eraser
  けいと      (keito)  yarn (毛糸)
     → あかい けいと        (akai keito)            red yarn
  けさ        (kesa)   this morning (今朝)
     → けさ は あめ          (kesa wa ame)           this morning it's raining

こ  ── THE PARK
  こうえん    (kōen)   park (公園)
     → こうえん で あそぶ    (kōen de asobu)         play in the park
  こども      (kodomo) child (子ども)
     → こども が わらう      (kodomo ga warau)       a child laughs
  こえ        (koe)    voice (声)
     → おおきい こえ        (ōkii koe)              a loud voice

REFRAIN
  か き く け こ  (ka ki ku ke ko)   the か-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WHAT THIS SONG QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Verbs (dictionary form, high-frequency): あらう wash, きる wear, かう buy, はく
  wear (shoes/trousers), のる ride/get on, あそぶ play, わらう laugh.
· Adjectives: きいろい yellow, あかい red, おおきい big.
· Particles: を (object, read "o"), の (of), に (in/at), で (at/in — place of action),
  は (topic, read "wa"), と (with/and).
· Word recombination WITH ROW 1: あめ (rain), いえ (house), そら (sky), えき (station),
  えんぴつ (pencil), and the verb かう all return in new sentences — that re-meeting is
  the practice.

These are context (comprehensible input), not the test — the TARGET is the か-row
words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 above (learn) → a BRIDGE that strings the errands
into one morning out → a REVIEW of the sentences only (sing along). A few extra
connective forms appear:
  だから     (dakara)   …so / therefore
  きて / のって / はいて  (kite / notte …)  …and then (て-form, chaining actions)
The day (bridge): 今朝の雨 rainy morning → dressed & shod → the car → the station
ticket → the shop → the park. Same words, now a flowing scene.

SIBLINGS (same five kana, different worlds): 02-kakikukeko-doubutsu (animals) and
02-kakikukeko-shizen (nature). Sing all three to read か行 cold.
