DEFINING THE MAIN VOICE (house-style.md, the strategic fork → option 3:
"one voice, many arrangements")

Plan:
1. Generate this song with a SINGLE clear voice in 2–3 of the styles above
   (A and B are the strongest contrast to judge on).
2. Pick the VOICE we want to hear across the whole series — judge the singer,
   not the arrangement.
3. Make a PERSONA from that take (Suno → Personas). This words-only, single-voice
   song is a GOOD Persona seed (no duet, no register jumps, plain neutral
   lyrics) — exactly what house-style.md says a seed should be.
4. From then on: hold the Persona CONSTANT, let the genre/arrangement vary per
   song (city pop one week, lo-fi the next) — that is how one artist ranges wide
   and still sounds like themselves.

Voice character to aim for: warm, mid-range, friendly, unmistakably CLEAR — a
patient teacher who is also pleasant to replay. Gender is open; test both if
unsure (A female vs A male) and let the ear decide.
