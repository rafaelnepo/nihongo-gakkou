まみむめも（しぜん）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 7 (ま行). NATURE variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the thing, then see it in the scene. Each ま-row word is taught through a short
sentence, gathered into one quiet day outdoors — pine and lake, water and harbour, field and
village, buds and birds, forest and mist. A calm sibling of the home and animals cuts.
Recombines earlier rows (みず, やま, はっぱ, ふね, えだ, はる) — the F-13 ladder. All hiragana;
kanji here only for meaning.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ま  ── PINE & LAKE
  まつ        (matsu)  pine tree (松)
     → みどりの まつ        (midori no matsu)       a green pine
  まりも      (marimo) marimo / algae ball (まりも)
     → みずうみ の まりも    (mizuumi no marimo)     marimo in the lake
  まき        (maki)   firewood (薪)
     → やま の まき          (yama no maki)          firewood in the mountains

み  ── WATER & HARBOUR
  みずうみ    (mizuumi) lake (湖)
     → しずかな みずうみ    (shizuka na mizuumi)    a quiet lake
  みず        (mizu)   water (水)
     → つめたい みず        (tsumetai mizu)         cold water
  みなと      (minato) harbour (港)
     → ふね の みなと        (fune no minato)        a harbour with boats

む  ── FIELD & VILLAGE
  むし        (mushi)  bug (虫)
     → はっぱ の むし        (happa no mushi)        a bug on a leaf
  むぎ        (mugi)   wheat (麦)
     → きいろい むぎ        (kiiroi mugi)           golden wheat
  むら        (mura)   village (村)
     → やま の むら          (yama no mura)          a mountain village

め  ── BUDS & BIRDS   (め here is 芽, a tree bud / sprout — not the eye)
  め          (me)     bud / sprout (芽)
     → き の め              (ki no me)              a tree bud
  めばえ      (mebae)  sprouting (芽生え)
     → はる の めばえ        (haru no mebae)         spring sprouts
  めじろ      (mejiro) white-eye bird (目白)
     → えだ の めじろ        (eda no mejiro)         a white-eye on a branch

も  ── FOREST & MIST
  もり        (mori)   forest (森)
     → ふかい もり          (fukai mori)            a deep forest
  もみじ      (momiji) maple leaves (紅葉)
     → あかい もみじ        (akai momiji)           red maple leaves
  もや        (moya)   mist / haze (靄)
     → あさ の もや          (asa no moya)           morning mist

REFRAIN
  ま み む め も  (ma mi mu me mo)   the ま-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Adjectives: みどりの green, しずかな quiet, つめたい cold, きいろい yellow, ふかい deep, あかい red.
· Nouns met in context: やま mountain, はっぱ leaf, ふね boat, えだ branch, はる spring, き tree.
· Particle: の (of) — the whole song is built on it.
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: みず, やま, はっぱ, ふね (は行), えだ (た行), はる all return.

The TARGET is the ま-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 (learn) → a BRIDGE through one day in nature → a REVIEW of
the sentences only (sing along).

SIBLINGS (same five kana): 07-mamimumemo-ouchi (home) and 07-mamimumemo-doubutsu (animals).
Sing all three to read ま行 cold.
