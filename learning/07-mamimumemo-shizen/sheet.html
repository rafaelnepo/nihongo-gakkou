01

The idea

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every target sits in a small image — しずかな みずうみ, き の め, ふかい もり — and it recombines with earlier rows (みず, やま, はっぱ, ふね, えだ, はる return), keeping the ladder (F-13).

The five-kana row ま み む め も closes every verse as the sung-together refrain (F-07). The 3-minute cut adds a day-in-nature bridge and a sentences-only review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the nature variation — the calm one, for koto × modern. ま行 has forest-and-water words (まつ, みずうみ, もり, もみじ) that suit it. め uses the tree-bud reading of 芽 (a sprout) — a tidy fit for a seasonal cut.
