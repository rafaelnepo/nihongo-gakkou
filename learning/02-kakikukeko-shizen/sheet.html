01

The idea

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every target sits in a small image — かぜ が ふく, もり の きのこ, とり の こえ — and it recombines with row 1 (うみ, そら, はっぱ return). The illustration carries the meaning; the sentence is the +1.

The five-kana row か き く け こ closes every verse as the sung-together refrain (F-07). The 3-minute cut adds a seasons-walk bridge and a sentences-only review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the nature variation — the calm one, written for a koto × modern arrangement rather than city pop. It also solves the か-row's hard け verse most gracefully: けさ (this morning) · けしき (scenery) · けむり (smoke) are fully on-theme, a quiet morning view. Sing it beside the animals and town cuts to read か行 cold.
