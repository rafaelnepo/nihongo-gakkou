かきくけこ（しぜん）— STYLE NOTES

THE CALM CUT. This nature variation is the one to render on KOTO × MODERN rather than
city pop — it suits the quiet outdoor scenes and gives the three か-row siblings some
sonic variety. Still the house FEMALE voice (F-11); still the same Persona, just a
calmer arrangement ("one voice, many arrangements", house-style.md). Custom mode; do
NOT specify gender on top of the Persona.

RECOMMENDED PROMPT (song.json styles[0]):
  modern Japanese pop with traditional koto, 90 BPM, single clear female vocalist,
  koto, shakuhachi accents, soft modern beat, warm bass, elegant and calm, dry forward
  vocal, extremely clear enunciation, no ad-libs.

TEMPO: ~90 (F-01 floor is 92 for the city-pop teach songs; the koto cut can sit a hair
under since it's calmer — keep it sing-along-able on first listen).

IF YOU PREFER CONSISTENCY over variety, city pop (styles[1]) also works — the animals
and town siblings use it. Alternatives kept for other content: acoustic, lo-fi. Full
strings in song.json.styles[].
