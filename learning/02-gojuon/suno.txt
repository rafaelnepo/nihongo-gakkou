五十音 — SUNO SCORE v1  (UNTESTED as of 2026-08-27)

WHY THIS IS ALL KANA: the original is dense with obscure kanji whose readings
Suno cannot possibly guess — 大角豆=ささげ, 蛞蝓=なめくじ, 啄木鳥=きつつき,
蝸牛=まいまい, 雷鳥=らいちょう. This is the maximum-danger case for findings.md
F-02. あいさつ needed three changed lines; this needs a full transliteration.
The learner sheet is where the kanji live. Here there are none.

PARTICLES CONVERTED: は→ワ, を→オ (F-02). Marked >< in the comments below.
ARCHAIC KANA KEPT AS WRITTEN: ヰ ヱ ヲ in stanza 10 — see README before deciding.

DESIGN: the five-kana run is the REFRAIN and it closes every verse, because
Suno puts both voices in unison on a verse's last line (F-07). That habit is
the whole point here — unison is where the listener joins in.

LENGTH: 42 lines. GENERATE IT IN ONE PASS. An earlier note here told you to
split this in half and join the two halves — that was wrong and is retracted
(findings.md F-09). Two generations share no voice, tempo or arrangement, so
there is nothing to merge. If one pass really does turn out to be too long,
use Suno's own EXTEND + "Get Whole Song", which conditions on the audio you
already have. Read the lyric sheet while it plays and check for dropped lines.
