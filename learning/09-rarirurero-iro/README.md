# 09-rarirurero-iro — ら行, the Colours & Pretty-Things variation

Gojuon **row 9 (ら り る れ ろ)**, taught through colours and pretty, quiet things — a white
orchid and a daffodil, a blue gentian and a little bell, lapis and a ruby, lotus and lace, a
candle's flame in a quiet alley. The **calm variation** of three ら-row songs (`ie` things ·
`doubutsu` animals · colours), for a koto × modern arrangement.

**Status: lyrics drafted (stage 1–2).** No audio yet. Written 2026-08-28. Recombines いけ,
いし, ひ, あき — the F-13 ladder. Pairs with the **Male (koto)** voice.

**Honest note:** ら行 has almost no nature vocabulary (and る is a near-wall kana), so this
stands in for the usual "nature" cut as a gentler colours & pretty-things theme — the
reachiest of the ら-row cuts.
