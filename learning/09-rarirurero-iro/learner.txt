らりるれろ（いろ）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 9 (ら行). COLOURS & PRETTY THINGS variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the thing, then see it. Each ら-row word is taught through a short sentence,
gathered into a calm, pretty scene — flowers and leaves, a gentian and a bell, gems, lotus
and lace, candlelight in a quiet place. One of THREE ら-row songs (things · animals · colours).
Recombines earlier rows (いけ, いし, ひ, あき) — the F-13 ladder.

HONEST NOTE: ら行 has almost no nature vocabulary (and る is a near-wall kana), so this stands
in for the usual "nature" cut as a gentler colours & pretty-things theme — the reachiest of the
ら-row cuts, held together by the koto's calm.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ら  ── FLOWERS & LEAVES
  らん        (ran)    orchid (蘭)
     → しろい らん          (shiroi ran)            a white orchid
  らっぱずいせん (rappa-zuisen) daffodil (ラッパ水仙)
     → きいろい らっぱずいせん (kiiroi rappa-zuisen) a yellow daffodil
  らくよう    (rakuyō) fallen leaves (落葉)
     → あき の らくよう      (aki no rakuyō)         autumn fallen leaves

り  ── GENTIAN & A BELL
  りんどう    (rindō)  gentian flower (竜胆)
     → あおい りんどう      (aoi rindō)             a blue gentian
  りん        (rin)    a little bell (鈴)
     → ちいさい りん        (chiisai rin)           a little bell
  りぼん      (ribon)  ribbon (リボン)
     → あかい りぼん        (akai ribon)            a red ribbon

る  ── GEMS   (る is a near-wall kana — few beginner words)
  るり        (ruri)   azure / lapis (瑠璃)
     → るり の いし          (ruri no ishi)          a lapis stone
  るびー      (rubī)   ruby (ルビー)
     → あかい るびー        (akai rubī)             a red ruby
  るーぺ      (rūpe)   loupe (ルーペ)
     → るーぺ で みる        (rūpe de miru)          look through a loupe

れ  ── LOTUS & LACE
  れんげ      (renge)  lotus flower (蓮華)
     → いけ の れんげ        (ike no renge)          lotus in the pond
  れんこん    (renkon) lotus root (蓮根)
     → しろい れんこん      (shiroi renkon)         white lotus root
  れーす      (rēsu)   lace (レース)
     → しろい れーす        (shiroi rēsu)           white lace

ろ  ── CANDLE & QUIET
  ろうそく    (rōsoku) candle (蝋燭)
     → ろうそく の ひ        (rōsoku no hi)          a candle's flame
  ろじ        (roji)   alley (路地)
     → しずかな ろじ        (shizuka na roji)       a quiet alley
  ろうか      (rōka)   corridor (廊下)
     → ながい ろうか        (nagai rōka)            a long corridor

REFRAIN
  ら り る れ ろ  (ra ri ru re ro)   the ら-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Verb: みる look. Adjectives: しろい white, きいろい yellow, あおい blue, あかい red,
  ちいさい small, しずかな quiet, ながい long.
· Nouns met in context: あき autumn, いし stone, いけ pond, ひ flame/light.
· Particles: の (of), で (with/by).
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: いけ (な行), いし (あ行), ひ (は行), あき all return.

The TARGET is the ら-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 (learn) → a colours BRIDGE → a REVIEW of the sentences only
(sing along).

SIBLINGS (same five kana): 09-rarirurero-ie (things) and 09-rarirurero-doubutsu (animals).
Sing all three to read ら行 cold.
