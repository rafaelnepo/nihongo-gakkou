Row 4 of the gojuon — た ち つ て と — through a day at home: an egg and tea, a desk and a letter, blocks and a drum, the clock and the night moon. Say the everyday thing, then use it. The home variation of three た-row songs (home · nature · animals).

01

The idea

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every target sits in a small action — たまご を たべる, てがみ を かく, とびら を あける — and it recombines with earlier rows (たべる, のむ, みる, あらう, ごはん, あかい return), keeping the ladder (F-13).

The five-kana row た ち つ て と closes every verse as the sung-together refrain (F-07). The 3-minute cut adds a day-at-home bridge and a sentences-only review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the home variation — た行 is rich in everyday objects, so the everyday cut is a cosy day indoors, morning to night. Pairs with the Female (city pop) voice.
