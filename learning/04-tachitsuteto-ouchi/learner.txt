たちつてと（おうち）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 4 (た行). HOME variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the everyday thing, then USE it. Each た-row word is taught through a short
sentence, gathered into a cosy day at home — morning, the table, play and study, and the
night. One of THREE た-row songs (home · nature · animals). Recombines earlier rows (たべる,
のむ, みる, あらう, ごはん, あかい) — the F-13 ladder. All hiragana; kanji here only for meaning.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
た  ── MORNING
  たまご      (tamago) egg (卵)
     → たまご を たべる      (tamago o taberu)       eat an egg
  たおる      (taoru)  towel (タオル)
     → しろい たおる        (shiroi taoru)          a white towel
  たいこ      (taiko)  drum (太鼓)
     → たいこ を たたく      (taiko o tataku)        beat the drum

ち  ── THE TABLE
  ちゃ        (cha)    tea (茶)
     → ちゃ を のむ          (cha o nomu)            drink tea
  ちゃわん    (chawan) rice bowl (茶碗)
     → ちゃわん の ごはん    (chawan no gohan)       rice in the bowl
  ちず        (chizu)  map (地図)
     → ちず を みる          (chizu o miru)          look at a map

つ  ── PLAY & STUDY
  つくえ      (tsukue) desk (机)
     → つくえ で かく        (tsukue de kaku)        write at the desk
  つみき      (tsumiki) building blocks (積み木)
     → つみき で あそぶ      (tsumiki de asobu)      play with blocks
  つき        (tsuki)  moon (月)
     → よる の つき          (yoru no tsuki)         the moon at night

て  ── HANDS & LETTERS
  て          (te)     hand (手)
     → て を あらう          (te o arau)             wash your hands
  てがみ      (tegami) letter (手紙)
     → てがみ を かく        (tegami o kaku)         write a letter
  てぶくろ    (tebukuro) gloves (手袋)
     → あたたかい てぶくろ  (atatakai tebukuro)     warm gloves

と  ── AROUND THE HOUSE
  とけい      (tokei)  clock (時計)
     → とけい を みる        (tokei o miru)          look at the clock
  とまと      (tomato) tomato (トマト)
     → あかい とまと        (akai tomato)           a red tomato
  とびら      (tobira) door (扉)
     → とびら を あける      (tobira o akeru)        open the door

REFRAIN
  た ち つ て と  (ta chi tsu te to)   the た-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Verbs: たべる eat, たたく beat/hit, のむ drink, みる look, かく write, あそぶ play,
  あらう wash, あける open.
· Adjectives: しろい white, あかい red, あたたかい warm.
· Particles: を (object, "o"), の (of), で (at/with — place or means), に? (—).
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: たべる, のむ, みる, あらう, ごはん, あかい all return.

The TARGET is the た-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 (learn) → a BRIDGE through one day at home → a REVIEW of
the sentences only (sing along). Extra words in the bridge: おひる (noon), しめる (to close).

SIBLINGS (same five kana): 04-tachitsuteto-shizen (nature) and 04-tachitsuteto-doubutsu
(animals). Sing all three to read た行 cold.
