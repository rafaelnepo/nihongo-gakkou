たちつてと（しぜん）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 4 (た行). NATURE variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the thing, then see it in the scene. Each た-row word is taught through a short
sentence, gathered into one quiet day outdoors — sun and mountain, earth and wings, moon and
soil, weather and a temple, sky and thorn. A calm sibling of the home and animals cuts.
Recombines earlier rows (あさ, やま, そら, はな, あおい, あかい) — the F-13 ladder. All
hiragana; kanji here only for meaning.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
た  ── SUN & MOUNTAIN
  たいよう    (taiyō)  the sun (太陽)
     → あかい たいよう      (akai taiyō)            the red sun
  たけ        (take)   bamboo (竹)
     → たかい たけ          (takai take)            tall bamboo
  たき        (taki)   waterfall (滝)
     → やま の たき          (yama no taki)          a mountain waterfall

ち  ── EARTH & WINGS
  ちきゅう    (chikyū) the earth (地球)
     → あおい ちきゅう      (aoi chikyū)            the blue earth
  ちょう      (chō)    butterfly (蝶)
     → ちょう が とぶ        (chō ga tobu)           a butterfly flies
  ちり        (chiri)  fallen petals / specks (散り)
     → はな の ちり          (hana no chiri)         fallen flower petals

つ  ── MOON & SOIL
  つき        (tsuki)  moon (月)
     → よる の つき          (yoru no tsuki)         the moon at night
  つち        (tsuchi) soil / earth (土)
     → くろい つち          (kuroi tsuchi)          black soil
  つゆ        (tsuyu)  dew (露)
     → はな の つゆ          (hana no tsuyu)         dew on a flower

て  ── WEATHER & A TEMPLE   (て is a thin row — carried with a ladybug and a temple)
  てんき      (tenki)  weather (天気)
     → いい てんき          (ii tenki)              nice weather
  てんとうむし (tentōmushi) ladybug (てんとう虫)
     → はっぱ の てんとうむし (happa no tentōmushi)  a ladybug on a leaf
  てら        (tera)   temple (寺)
     → やま の てら          (yama no tera)          a mountain temple

と  ── SKY & THORN
  とり        (tori)   bird (鳥)
     → そら の とり          (sora no tori)          a bird in the sky
  とんぼ      (tonbo)  dragonfly (蜻蛉)
     → あかい とんぼ        (akai tonbo)            a red dragonfly
  とげ        (toge)   thorn (棘)
     → ばら の とげ          (bara no toge)          a rose's thorn

REFRAIN
  た ち つ て と  (ta chi tsu te to)   the た-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Adjectives: あかい red, たかい tall, あおい blue, くろい black, いい good/nice.
· Nouns met in context: やま mountain, はな flower, はっぱ leaf, そら sky, ばら rose, よる night.
· Particles: の (of), が (subject) — a quiet, descriptive grammar.
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: あさ, やま, そら, はな return; あおい/あかい reused.

The TARGET is the た-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 (learn) → a BRIDGE through one day in nature → a REVIEW of
the sentences only (sing along).

SIBLINGS (same five kana): 04-tachitsuteto-ouchi (home) and 04-tachitsuteto-doubutsu
(animals). Sing all three to read た行 cold.
