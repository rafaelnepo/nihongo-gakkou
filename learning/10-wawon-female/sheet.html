01

The idea

わ行 is the last row, and it is special. Only わ is a word-starter (わに, わし, わた…). を is only the object particle — essentially never begins a word, always read "o" (findings F-16). ん never starts a word either; it only ends syllables and words (ぱん, みかん, きりん). So you can't make 3 groups × 3 words — わ行 needs a different design.

The design: a graduation "last sounds" song. A わ teach (six わ-words, each in a small sentence — same onset blends, so context is essential, F-10), then a を beat that shows the joiner doing its job in already-known sentences (りんご を たべる, うた を うたう), then a ん beat for the finisher (おいしい ぱん, あまい みかん), and a whole-gojuon REVIEW — one word from every earlier row (あさ・かに・さかな・つき・ねこ・ほし・みかん・やぎ・りんご) recombined into a ぜんぶ よめた！ victory lap. The ultimate F-13 payoff.

This is the female / city-pop cut. The finale is written once and rendered in all three house voices — Female (city pop), Male (koto), Kids (graduation singalong) — Strategy B in personas.md, so each per-voice playlist ends on the same capstone. The refrain わ を ん is the whole point and lands last in every verse (F-07); the closing sweep あ か さ た な は ま や ら わ を ん is the series taking one last breath.
