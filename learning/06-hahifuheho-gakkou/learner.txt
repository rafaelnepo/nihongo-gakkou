はひふへほ（つくる）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 6 (は行). MAKING variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the thing, then USE it. Each は-row word is taught through a short sentence,
gathered into a busy day of making — cut, fold, paint, blow a whistle, read a book. One of
THREE は-row songs (making · nature · animals). Recombines earlier rows (きる, あける, かく,
ふく, よる) — the F-13 ladder. All hiragana; kanji here only for meaning.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
は  ── CUT & FOLD
  はさみ      (hasami) scissors (鋏)
     → はさみ で きる        (hasami de kiru)        cut with scissors
  はこ        (hako)   box (箱)
     → はこ を あける        (hako o akeru)          open the box
  はた        (hata)   flag (旗)
     → あかい はた          (akai hata)             a red flag

ひ  ── PAPER & LIGHT
  ひこうき    (hikōki) airplane (飛行機)
     → かみ の ひこうき      (kami no hikōki)        a paper airplane
  ひも        (himo)   string (紐)
     → ながい ひも          (nagai himo)            a long string
  ひかり      (hikari) light (光)
     → あかるい ひかり      (akarui hikari)         a bright light

ふ  ── BRUSH & WHISTLE
  ふで        (fude)   brush (筆)
     → ふで で かく          (fude de kaku)          paint with a brush
  ふうせん    (fūsen)  balloon (風船)
     → まるい ふうせん      (marui fūsen)           a round balloon
  ふえ        (fue)    whistle / flute (笛)
     → ふえ を ふく          (fue o fuku)            blow a whistle

へ  ── THE ROOM   (へ is a thin row — a room, a fence, a loofah carry it)
  へや        (heya)   room (部屋)
     → あかるい へや        (akarui heya)           a bright room
  へい        (hei)    fence / wall (塀)
     → しろい へい          (shiroi hei)            a white fence
  へちま      (hechima) loofah gourd (へちま)
     → みどりの へちま      (midori no hechima)     a green loofah

ほ  ── READ & TIDY
  ほん        (hon)    book (本)
     → ほん を よむ          (hon o yomu)            read a book
  ほうき      (hōki)   broom (箒)
     → ほうき で はく        (hōki de haku)          sweep with a broom
  ほし        (hoshi)  star (星)
     → よる の ほし          (yoru no hoshi)         stars at night

REFRAIN
  は ひ ふ へ ほ  (ha hi fu he ho)   the は-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Verbs: きる cut, あける open, かく write/paint, ふく blow, よむ read, はく sweep.
· Adjectives: あかい red, ながい long, あかるい bright, まるい round, しろい white, みどりの green.
· Particles: で (with/by), を (object, "o"), の (of).
· Note the two READINGS of は: the row kana は inside words (はこ, はた) reads "ha"; only the
  topic particle は reads "wa" (findings F-02). This song's sentences avoid the particle.

The TARGET is the は-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 (learn) → a BRIDGE through one day of making → a REVIEW of
the sentences only (sing along).

SIBLINGS (same five kana): 06-hahifuheho-shizen (nature) and 06-hahifuheho-doubutsu (animals).
Sing all three to read は行 cold.
