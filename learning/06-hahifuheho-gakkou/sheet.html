Row 6 of the gojuon — は ひ ふ へ ほ — through a day of making things: cut with scissors, fold a paper airplane, paint with a brush, blow a whistle, read a book under the stars. Say the thing, then use it. The making variation of three は-row songs (making · nature · animals).

01

The idea

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every target sits in a small action — はさみ で きる, ふで で かく, ほん を よむ — and it recombines with earlier rows (きる, あける, かく, ふく, あかい, しろい, みどり, よる return), keeping the ladder (F-13).

The five-kana row は ひ ふ へ ほ closes every verse as the sung-together refrain (F-07). The 3-minute cut adds a craft-day bridge and a sentences-only review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the making variation — は行 is rich in craft and classroom things, so the everyday cut is a busy day of scissors, brushes and books. Honest note: へ is a thin row (a room, a fence, a loofah). Pairs with the Female (city pop) voice.
