01

The idea

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every animal is taught in a small action — たぬき が いる, つる が おどる — and it recombines with earlier rows (しか, さる, すな, す, うみ, そら + verbs いる・とぶ・およぐ return), keeping the ladder (F-13).

The five-kana row た ち つ て と closes every verse as the sung-together refrain (F-07). The 3-minute cut adds a zoo-day bridge and a sentences-only review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the animals variation — た行 has good animal coverage (たぬき, つる, とら, とんぼ…). Honest note: ち and て are the thin rows, so they use a garden eel, a gibbon, and a paw (さる の て) as small detours. Pairs with the kids voice — a lively zoo dance-along.
