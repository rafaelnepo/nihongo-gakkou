やゆよ（しぜん）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 8 (や行, a SHORT row). NATURE variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the thing, then see it in the scene. The ya-row has only THREE kana (や ゆ よ),
so this teaches 3 groups of 3 words (9 targets), refrain や ゆ よ. A calm sibling of the home
and fun cuts. Recombines earlier rows (かわ, にわ, ほし, はる) — the F-13 ladder. All hiragana.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
や  ── MOUNTAIN & RIVER
  やま        (yama)   mountain (山)
     → たかい やま          (takai yama)            a tall mountain
  やなぎ      (yanagi) willow (柳)
     → かわ の やなぎ        (kawa no yanagi)        a willow by the river
  やぶ        (yabu)   thicket (藪)
     → みどりの やぶ        (midori no yabu)        a green thicket

ゆ  ── SNOW & SUNSET
  ゆき        (yuki)   snow (雪)
     → しろい ゆき          (shiroi yuki)           white snow
  ゆり        (yuri)   lily (百合)
     → にわ の ゆり          (niwa no yuri)          a lily in the garden
  ゆうやけ    (yūyake) sunset (夕焼け)
     → あかい ゆうやけ      (akai yūyake)           a red sunset

よ  ── NIGHT SKY & CLOVER
  よぞら      (yozora) night sky (夜空)
     → ほし の よぞら        (hoshi no yozora)       a starry night sky
  よもぎ      (yomogi) mugwort (蓬)
     → はる の よもぎ        (haru no yomogi)        spring mugwort
  よつば      (yotsuba) four-leaf clover (四つ葉)
     → よつば を さがす      (yotsuba o sagasu)      look for a four-leaf clover

REFRAIN
  や ゆ よ  (ya yu yo)   the ya-row (short)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 9 WORDS:
· Verb: さがす search/look for. Adjectives: たかい tall, みどりの green, しろい white, あかい red.
· Nouns met in context: かわ river, にわ garden, ほし star, はる spring.
· Particles: の (of), を (object, always "o"; never swap — F-16).
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: かわ (か行), にわ, ほし (は行), はる all return.

The TARGET is the や-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE CUTS: v2 = teach only (~0:45); v3 = teach → bridge → review (~2:00). A short row makes a
short song — that's fine.

SIBLINGS (same three kana): 08-yayuyo-ie (home) and 08-yayuyo-asobi (a fun day out).
Sing all three to read や行 cold.
