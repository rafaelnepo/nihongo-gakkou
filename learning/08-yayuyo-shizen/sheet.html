01

The idea

や行 is a short row — three kana (や ゆ よ; い/え borrowed from あ行) — so this is 3 groups of 3 words (9 targets), refrain や ゆ よ, and a naturally shorter song.

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every target sits in a small image — かわ の やなぎ, ほし の よぞら — recombining earlier rows (かわ, にわ, ほし, はる return; F-13). Row closes every verse (F-07); the longer cut adds a bridge + review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the nature variation — the calm one, for koto × modern. や行 is rich in seasonal nature (やなぎ, ゆき, ゆうやけ, よぞら). Pairs with the Male (koto) voice.
