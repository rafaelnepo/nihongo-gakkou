あいさつ — SUNO SCORE v3  (UNTESTED as of 2026-08-27)

WHAT v2 GOT RIGHT: two voices appeared. The doubling worked.
WHAT v2 GOT WRONG, TWICE:
  · Suno ignored the per-block roles and imposed its own duet shape —
    male part, female part, both in unison on the verse's last line (F-07).
  · It sang よるの あいさつ twice and dropped ひるの あいさつ entirely (F-08),
    because v2 put two lines differing by ONE MORA next to each other.

v3 CHANGES, both structural:
  1. STOP FIGHTING THE UNISON. The phrase that most deserves to be sung by
     everyone now sits LAST in each verse, so Suno's own habit recruits the
     listener instead of working against the design. (F-07 rule.)
  2. NEAR-IDENTICAL LINES ARE NO LONGER ADJACENT. ひるの / よるの are separated
     by their own answers, so the frame and the fill alternate the way they do
     on the learner sheet. Nothing collapses into its neighbour.
  3. The HOOK is doubled (identical, harmless if repeated or dropped).
     The VERSE lines are NOT doubled — each carries unique meaning, and a
     doubled unique line is an invitation to eat one of them.
