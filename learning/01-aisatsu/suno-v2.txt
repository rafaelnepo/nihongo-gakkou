あいさつ — SUNO SCORE v2  (DUET FIX — UNTESTED as of 2026-08-27)
CHANGE FROM v1: every line is doubled, so each voice holds TWO lines before
handing over. v1 alternated every single line, which is finer than Suno can
switch — it either keeps one voice or degrades into 朗読劇 (a reading play).
Practitioner consensus is that a part change must sit in a 2-line block.
The doubling is not a compromise: it doubles the repetition, which is the
method. See ../findings.md F-04.
ALSO: named-voice tags per section, and a duet declaration at the top.
