さしすせそ（しぜん）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 3 (さ行). NATURE variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the thing, then see it in the scene. Each さ-row word is taught through a short
sentence, gathered into one quiet walk through the seasons. A calm sibling of the mealtime
and animals cuts — same five kana, fresh words. Recombines earlier rows (あめ, あき, うみ,
そら, かぜ, はる) — the F-13 ladder. All hiragana; kanji here only for meaning.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
さ  ── THE HILLSIDE
  さくら      (sakura) cherry blossom (桜)
     → さくら の はな        (sakura no hana)        cherry blossoms
  さか        (saka)   slope / hill (坂)
     → ながい さか          (nagai saka)            a long slope
  さんぽ      (sanpo)  a walk (散歩)
     → やま の さんぽ        (yama no sanpo)         a mountain walk

し  ── WATER
  しま        (shima)  island (島)
     → うみ の しま          (umi no shima)          an island in the sea
  しずく      (shizuku) droplet (雫)
     → あめ の しずく        (ame no shizuku)        a raindrop
  しみず      (shimizu) spring water (清水)
     → つめたい しみず      (tsumetai shimizu)      cold spring water

す  ── EARTH & FLOWERS
  すな        (suna)   sand (砂)
     → しろい すな          (shiroi suna)           white sand
  すみれ      (sumire) violet (すみれ)
     → むらさきの すみれ    (murasaki no sumire)    a purple violet
  すすき      (susuki) pampas grass (すすき)
     → あき の すすき        (aki no susuki)         autumn pampas grass

せ  ── BROOK & CICADA   (せ is a hard row — nature carries it best of the three cuts)
  せせらぎ    (seseragi) a babbling brook (せせらぎ)
     → やま の せせらぎ      (yama no seseragi)      a mountain brook
  せみ        (semi)   cicada (蝉)
     → なつ の せみ          (natsu no semi)         a summer cicada
  せかい      (sekai)  the world (世界)
     → ひろい せかい        (hiroi sekai)           a wide world

そ  ── SKY & WIND
  そら        (sora)   sky (空)
     → あおい そら          (aoi sora)              the blue sky
  そよかぜ    (soyokaze) a gentle breeze (そよ風)
     → はる の そよかぜ      (haru no soyokaze)      a spring breeze
  そこ        (soko)   the depths / bottom (底)
     → うみ の そこ          (umi no soko)           the depths of the sea

REFRAIN
  さ し す せ そ  (sa shi su se so)   the さ-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Adjectives: ながい long, つめたい cold, しろい white, むらさきの purple, ひろい wide,
  あおい blue, すずしい cool.
· Nouns met in context: はな flower, やま mountain, みち path.
· Particle: の (of) — the whole song is built on it (a quiet, descriptive grammar).
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: あめ, あき, うみ, そら, かぜ, はる all return.

The TARGET is the さ-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 (learn) → a BRIDGE walking the scenes into one seasonal
day → a REVIEW of the sentences only (sing along).

SIBLINGS (same five kana): 03-sashisuseso-gohan (mealtime) and 03-sashisuseso-doubutsu
(animals). Sing all three to read さ行 cold.
