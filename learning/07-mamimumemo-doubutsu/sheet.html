The idea

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every animal is taught in a small action — まぐろ が およぐ, もぐら が いる — and it recombines with earlier rows (うみ, いけ, つち, はな, はっぱ, えだ + verbs およぐ・とぶ・いる return), keeping the ladder (F-13).

The five-kana row ま み む め も closes every verse as the sung-together refrain (F-07). The 3-minute cut adds a zoo-day bridge and a sentences-only review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the animals variation — ま行 is a clean animal row (a real creature in every kana, no detours): fish, bees, bugs, a mole, a butterfly. Pairs with the kids voice — a lively land-sea-and-sky dance-along.
