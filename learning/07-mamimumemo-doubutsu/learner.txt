まみむめも（どうぶつ）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 7 (ま行). ANIMALS variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the animal, then see it DO something. Each ま-row word is taught through a short
sentence, gathered into a lively land-sea-and-sky day. One of THREE ま-row songs (home ·
nature · animals). Recombines earlier rows (うみ, いけ, つち, えだ, はっぱ + verbs およぐ・
とぶ・いる) — the F-13 ladder. All hiragana; kanji here only for meaning.

NOTE: ま行 is a clean animal row — a real creature in every kana (no detours this time),
though a few are aquarium/insect favourites (a sunfish, a bagworm, a flying squirrel).

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ま  ── IN THE SEA & SKY
  まぐろ      (maguro) tuna (鮪)
     → まぐろ が およぐ      (maguro ga oyogu)       a tuna swims
  まんぼう    (manbō)  ocean sunfish (翻車魚)
     → まんぼう は まるい    (manbō wa marui)        a sunfish is round
  まがも      (magamo) mallard duck (真鴨)
     → まがも が とぶ        (magamo ga tobu)        a mallard flies

み  ── BEES & OWLS
  みつばち    (mitsubachi) honeybee (蜜蜂)
     → みつばち が とぶ      (mitsubachi ga tobu)    a honeybee flies
  みみずく    (mimizuku) horned owl (木菟)
     → みみずく が いる      (mimizuku ga iru)       there's a horned owl
  みのむし    (minomushi) bagworm (蓑虫)
     → えだ の みのむし      (eda no minomushi)      a bagworm on a branch

む  ── CREEPY CRAWLIES
  むし        (mushi)  bug (虫)
     → ちいさい むし        (chiisai mushi)         a little bug
  むかで      (mukade) centipede (百足)
     → むかで が あるく      (mukade ga aruku)       a centipede walks
  むささび    (musasabi) flying squirrel (鼯鼠)
     → むささび が とぶ      (musasabi ga tobu)      a flying squirrel glides

め  ── FISH, BIRD & COW
  めだか      (medaka) killifish (めだか)
     → めだか が およぐ      (medaka ga oyogu)       a killifish swims
  めじろ      (mejiro) white-eye (bird) (目白)
     → めじろ が なく        (mejiro ga naku)        a white-eye chirps
  めうし      (meushi) cow (雌牛)
     → しろい めうし        (shiroi meushi)         a white cow

も  ── MOLE & BUTTERFLY
  もぐら      (mogura) mole (土竜)
     → もぐら が いる        (mogura ga iru)         there's a mole
  もず        (mozu)   shrike (bird) (百舌)
     → もず が なく          (mozu ga naku)          a shrike cries
  もんしろちょう (monshirochō) cabbage white butterfly (紋白蝶)
     → もんしろちょう が とぶ (monshirochō ga tobu)  a cabbage white flies

REFRAIN
  ま み む め も  (ma mi mu me mo)   the ま-row
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 15 WORDS:
· Verbs: およぐ swim, とぶ fly, いる be, あるく walk, なく cry/chirp.
· Adjectives: まるい round, ちいさい small, しろい white. Particles: が (subject), は (topic,
  "wa"), の (of).
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: うみ, いけ (な行), つち (た行), えだ (た行), はっぱ all return.

The TARGET is the ま-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE ~3-MIN CUT (suno-v3): PART 1 (learn) → a zoo-day BRIDGE → a REVIEW of the sentences only
(sing along). Extra words in the bridge: なか (inside), うえ (on top).

SIBLINGS (same five kana): 07-mamimumemo-ouchi (home) and 07-mamimumemo-shizen (nature).
Sing all three to read ま行 cold.
