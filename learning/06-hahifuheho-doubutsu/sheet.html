Animals aplenty: a bee, a pigeon and a hedgehog, a sheep and a chick, an owl and a pufferfish, a snake, a firefly and a polar bear. Say the animal, then watch it do something. The animals cut of three は-row songs.

01

The idea

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every animal is taught in a small action — はち が とぶ, ほたる が ひかる — and it recombines with earlier rows (ねこ, いぬ, いけ, うみ, なつ, よる + verbs とぶ・いる・はしる return), keeping the ladder (F-13).

The five-kana row は ひ ふ へ ほ closes every verse as the sung-together refrain (F-07). The 3-minute cut adds a zoo-day bridge and a sentences-only review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the animals variation — は行 has strong animal coverage (a bee, a hedgehog, a sheep, an owl, a pufferfish, a polar bear). Only へ is thin: へび (snake) is the one へ animal, so its verse adds a cat on a fence and a dog in a room. Pairs with the kids voice — a lively zoo dance-along.
