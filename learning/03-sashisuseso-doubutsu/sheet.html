Row 3 of the gojuon — さ し す せ そ — through animals: a monkey and a rhino, a deer and a zebra, a sparrow and a cricket, a cicada and a walrus. Say the animal, then watch it do something. The animals variation of three さ-row songs — the toughest row for animals, so it leans on a couple of honest detours.

01

The idea

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every animal is taught in a small action — さる が あそぶ, すずめ が とぶ — and it recombines with earlier rows (くま, いぬ, うみ, and the verbs あそぶ・いる・とぶ・なく return), keeping the ladder (F-13).

The five-kana row さ し す せ そ closes every verse as the sung-together refrain (F-07). The 3-minute cut adds a zoo-day bridge and a sentences-only review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the animals variation. Honest note: さ行 is the hardest row for animals yet — せ has few (せみ cicada, せいうち walrus, then せなか 'a back'), and そ has essentially none, so the そ verse teaches position words (そら sky, そこ depths, そと outside) with an animal in each scene. Two documented detours, in the spirit of か行's け and あ行's え. Pairs with the kids voice — a lively zoo dance-along.
