01

The idea

や行 is a short row — three kana (や ゆ よ) — so 3 groups of 3 words (9 targets), refrain や ゆ よ, a naturally shorter song.

Words alone blend (findings F-10), so every target sits in a small action — やきゅう を する, ようちえん へ いく — recombining earlier rows (する, まつり, うみ, いく return; F-13). Row closes every verse (F-07); the longer cut adds a bridge + review, not a loop (F-12).

This is the fun day out variation — it takes the animals slot for this short row, because ゆ and よ have no beginner animals, so a playful outing (baseball, the fair, the amusement park, a yacht) fits the kids voice better. This is also the one song with a へ direction particle — ようちえん へ いく — read "e".
