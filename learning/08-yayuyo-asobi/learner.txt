やゆよ（あそび）— LEARNER SHEET  (learning/ strand — PUBLIC series)
Gojuon ROW 8 (や行, a SHORT row). FUN-DAY variation. Written 2026-08-28.

CONCEPT: say the thing, then USE it. The ya-row has only THREE kana (や ゆ よ), so this
teaches 3 groups of 3 words (9 targets), refrain や ゆ よ. This cut takes the "animals slot"
as a fun day out — ゆ and よ have no beginner animals, so a playful outing suits the kids voice
better. Recombines earlier rows (まつり, うみ, いく) — the F-13 ladder. All hiragana.

Format: `WORD  (romaji) meaning`  then  `→ SENTENCE  (romaji)  meaning`.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
や  ── AT THE FAIR
  やきゅう    (yakyū)  baseball (野球)
     → やきゅう を する      (yakyū o suru)          play baseball
  やぎ        (yagi)   goat (山羊)
     → やぎ が いる          (yagi ga iru)           there's a goat
  やたい      (yatai)  festival stall (屋台)
     → まつり の やたい      (matsuri no yatai)      a festival stall

ゆ  ── THE AMUSEMENT PARK
  ゆうえんち  (yūenchi) amusement park (遊園地)
     → たのしい ゆうえんち  (tanoshii yūenchi)      a fun amusement park
  ゆきだるま  (yukidaruma) snowman (雪だるま)
     → おおきい ゆきだるま  (ōkii yukidaruma)       a big snowman
  ゆびわ      (yubiwa) ring (指輪)
     → ひかる ゆびわ        (hikaru yubiwa)         a shiny ring

よ  ── OFF WE GO
  よっと      (yotto)  yacht (ヨット)
     → うみ の よっと        (umi no yotto)          a yacht on the sea
  ようちえん  (yōchien) kindergarten (幼稚園)
     → ようちえん へ いく    (yōchien e iku)         go to kindergarten  ← へ read "e"
  ようかん    (yōkan)  yokan / sweet jelly (羊羹)
     → あまい ようかん      (amai yōkan)            sweet yokan

REFRAIN
  や ゆ よ  (ya yu yo)   the ya-row (short)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUIETLY TEACHES BEYOND THE 9 WORDS:
· Verbs: する do/play, いる be, いく go. Adjectives: たのしい fun, おおきい big, ひかる shiny,
  あまい sweet.
· Particles: を (object, always "o"; never swap — F-16), の (of), へ (direction, read "e" —
  ようちえん へ いく). This is the ya-row's one へ particle.
· Recombination WITH EARLIER ROWS: まつり, うみ, いく (あ行) all return.

The TARGET is the や-row words; the sentence, the audio and the illustration carry the rest.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE CUTS: v2 = teach only (~0:45); v3 = teach → bridge → review (~2:00). A short row makes a
short song — that's fine.

SIBLINGS (same three kana): 08-yayuyo-ie (home) and 08-yayuyo-shizen (nature).
Sing all three to read や行 cold.
